An AI can sound certain, and still invent the answer. In a 2026 Nature study, researchers asked three popular AI models a simple question: What does PGGB stand for? ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek each produced a confident definition. All three were wrong. The paper explains one reason: many evaluations reward correct answers, but do not adequately reward saying, I don't know. So when a model is uncertain, guessing can look better than admitting it. The lesson? Treat fluent answers as claims, not proof. Check the sources before you trust them. Follow ShortPrompt for more real tests of AI.
