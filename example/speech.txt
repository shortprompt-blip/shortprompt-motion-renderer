OpenAI has introduced Dots, a new kind of personal AI agent designed to keep working on your goals in the background.

Instead of asking for a single answer and starting over every time, you give a Dot a goal and let it work through the task.

In this example, we give it a simple project brief and connect the sources it needs.

Dots can then organize information, identify the important tasks, and prepare a concise action list.

The key idea is that the agent keeps working toward the goal instead of waiting for you to guide every single step.

And before actions are taken, permissions and connected services can be reviewed.

Once the work is complete, the result is ready for you to inspect and approve.

That is the interesting part of Dots: AI that works in the background, while you stay in control.
