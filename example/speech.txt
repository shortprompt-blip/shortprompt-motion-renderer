Can you unlock sixty frames per second in Grand Theft Auto six on PlayStation five? Here is the honest answer, and the settings that are still worth checking.

Rockstar’s published target for GTA six on current generation consoles is thirty frames per second. The studio has not announced a sixty FPS performance mode. And Rockstar’s technology production director later explained that even PlayStation five Pro’s processor is not powerful enough to run the game’s complex simulation at sixty frames per second with its current visual design.

So there is no hidden PS5 menu, secret graphics option, or TV setting that turns GTA six into a sixty FPS game. Be careful with videos claiming a guaranteed unlock.

But you can still optimize your display experience. On PS5, open Settings, then Screen and Video, then Video Output. If your TV supports it, set VRR to Automatic and 120 Hertz Output to Automatic. These options allow compatible games and displays to use variable refresh or high refresh output. They do not generate extra game frames when GTA six is running at thirty.

Enable ALLM if your television supports it, or manually select Game Mode on your TV to reduce input lag. Use the HDMI port recommended for high bandwidth features and the supplied Ultra High Speed cable.

Bottom line: you can improve display responsiveness and compatibility, but you cannot force GTA six to sixty FPS on PS5. Check official updates, and don’t confuse a 120 Hertz signal with sixty frames rendered by the game.
