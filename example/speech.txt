AI agents need more than a clever answer. They need to keep working through multi-step tasks without wasting time or compute. NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning is a thirty-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model designed for efficient, long-running agent workloads. Think coding workflows, research tasks, and systems where several agents coordinate. It is an open model, so developers can adapt it instead of treating it like a sealed chatbot.
