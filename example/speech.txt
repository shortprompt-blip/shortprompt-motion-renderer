If your game, Discord and microphone are mixed together, changing one volume can ruin the others. Here is how to separate them in OBS Studio.

This setup requires Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11, and OBS 28 or newer.

Add each sound as its own source. Use Application Audio Capture for the game and Discord, then add your microphone as Audio Input Capture. OBS 30.1 or later can also capture audio through Game Capture or Window Capture.

Open the Audio Mixer, choose Advanced Audio Properties, and assign the tracks.

Use Track 1 for the complete mix. Keep all three sources checked on Track 1. Then assign Discord to Track 2, game audio to Track 3, and your microphone to Track 4.

Go to Settings, Output, switch to Advanced mode, and enable those recording tracks. Record to MKV, then remux to MP4 if needed.

Separate tracks let you edit each source later. Your live stream still needs a complete mix. Most standard video players play only one track, so check the recording in an editor.

Make a short test. Lower Discord, mute the game, and confirm each track behaves independently. Follow for more OBS creator fixes.
