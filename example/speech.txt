Your Reel might be perfectly fine… but Instagram may not recommend it to new people. Check this first.

Open your profile and go to Settings and activity.

Then open Account Status.

This is where Instagram shows whether there are issues affecting your account or content, including recommendation eligibility.

Look for Recommendation eligibility and check whether your content can be recommended to people who don’t follow you.

If you want reach beyond your existing audience, this is one status worth checking before blaming the algorithm.
