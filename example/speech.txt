How much does YouTube pay for one hundred thousand views? It depends on your niche.

YouTube has no fixed rate. For long-form videos, RPM means estimated creator revenue per thousand views.

A 2026 third-party sample of 300 channels reported median RPMs of ten dollars and twenty-two cents for education and science, two dollars and ninety-eight cents for lifestyle, two dollars and thirty-three cents for tech, and two dollars and five cents for gaming.

Using those sample medians, one hundred thousand views works out to about one thousand and twenty-two dollars for education, two hundred and ninety-eight for lifestyle, two hundred and thirty-three for tech, and two hundred and five for gaming.

Same views, very different estimates.

But these are not official YouTube rates or guaranteed payouts. Audience location, season, video length and monetized views can change the result. Some niche samples are small. And Shorts use a different RPM calculation, so don't compare them directly with long-form.
