Your GPU is at ninety-nine percent while gaming. Is that bad? Usually, no. It often means the graphics card is fully occupied doing the work needed to render frames. That can be a normal GPU-limited situation. The important question is whether performance is meeting your target. If it is, you may not need to change anything. If it is not, try lowering demanding graphics settings and compare frame time. Do not chase one-hundred-percent CPU and GPU usage just for the numbers.
