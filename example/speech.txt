Here’s a practical way to remix a song with AI.

First, start with the original track and extract the useful musical information: tempo, key, energy, and the structure of the arrangement.

In this example, the source is one hundred and twenty-four BPM in A minor.

Now define what should change, instead of asking for a completely different song. Keep the main hook, tighten the drums, and push the energy toward a dance remix.

That distinction matters because the AI now has a constrained transformation target.

Next, rebuild the arrangement around those constraints. The important part is not the label on the screen — it is the change in the musical structure: the rhythm becomes denser, the energy rises, and the drop gets a new role.

Finally, compare the original with the remix.

The goal is not simply to make something different. A useful AI remix keeps the identity of the source while deliberately changing the parts you asked it to change.

That is the workflow: analyze, define the target, generate the transformation, and then compare the result.
