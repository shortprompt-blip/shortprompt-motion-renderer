Want cleaner audio for YouTube, streaming, or gaming? Set up your USB microphone in four quick steps.

First, connect it directly to your PC. Place it about ten to twenty centimetres from your mouth, and speak toward the front of the microphone.

Open Windows Settings, choose System, then Sound. Under Input, select your microphone, run the microphone test, and adjust the input level until your voice is clear without distortion.

Next, open the microphone’s companion app. Run its gain setup, check your headphone monitoring, and enable voice cleanup only if your room has noticeable background noise.

In OBS, add an Audio Input Capture source and choose your microphone. Open Filters and add Noise Suppression with RNNoise to reduce steady fan or room noise.

Watch the audio meter while speaking. Aim for peaks around minus twelve to minus six decibels, and never let your voice hit the red.

Finally, record a short test and listen back with headphones. Keep your game audio and microphone balanced separately.
