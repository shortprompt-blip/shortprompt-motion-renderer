Want more people to follow you from your Instagram profile? Start with your bio. First, clearly say what you do and who you help. Avoid vague phrases like ‘I create content’ and explain the value you provide. Then give people a reason to follow you, such as useful tips, strategies, or results they can expect. Finally, make sure your profile link matches your bio. Your bio should answer three questions in seconds: who are you, what do you offer, and why should someone follow you?
