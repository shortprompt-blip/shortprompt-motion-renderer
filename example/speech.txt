Thinking about creating a second Instagram account?

There is a good reason creators are starting to separate content across different accounts: audience intent.

Take a food creator. One account might focus on recipes and cooking. A second account could focus on restaurant reviews or local recommendations.

The content is related, but the audience may be different.

So how do you know if you need a second Instagram account?

First, check whether the new content attracts a genuinely different audience.

Second, ask whether people who follow your main account actually want both types of content.

And third, make sure the second account has enough content to stay active.

If the audiences overlap, keep one account. Splitting too early can divide your momentum and double your workload.

But if the audiences, content and goals are clearly different, a second account can make your positioning much clearer.
