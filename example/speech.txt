Can you make an AI video without paying a single dollar?

Here’s the three-step workflow.

First, use Google AI Studio to turn one idea into a short script and a clear visual prompt.

Next, try Luma Dream Machine’s free plan to generate a short video clip. Check the current limits before you start.

Then, use TTSMaker to create the voiceover and download the audio.

That’s the pipeline: prompt, video, voice.

But remember: free doesn’t mean unlimited, and Luma’s free plan has personal-use restrictions and a watermark. Don’t assume you can monetize every output.
