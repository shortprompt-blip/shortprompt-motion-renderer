Want to record PS5 gameplay for YouTube? You can start with the console itself: press the Create button, then choose Start New Recording. For longer or more flexible production, use an external capture card and OBS on a computer.

First, connect the PS5 directly to your display and turn HDCP off in Settings, System, HDMI. Then connect PS5 HDMI to the card's HDMI In, the card's HDMI Out to your display, and USB to your computer.

In OBS, add a Video Capture Device source and select the capture card. Check the resolution, frame rate and audio before recording.

Here are two options: the UGREEN capture card for a simpler entry-level setup, and the Elgato option for a more dedicated capture workflow. Check each listing's exact capture specs before buying.
