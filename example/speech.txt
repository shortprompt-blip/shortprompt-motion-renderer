Getting views but losing people too quickly? Your video may have a retention problem.

Open YouTube Studio, select your video, open Analytics, and check Engagement.

Look at the audience retention graph. A gradual decline is normal, but sharp dips show moments where viewers skipped or stopped watching.

Now find where the biggest drop happens.

If it happens at the beginning, your hook may not match the promise of the title and thumbnail.

If viewers leave later, look at what changed in that section: the pacing, the explanation, or the payoff.

Don't just look at average view duration. Find where viewers leave, then fix that moment in your next video.
