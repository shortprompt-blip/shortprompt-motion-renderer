Most creators guess what to post next. TikTok can help you start with what people are already searching for.
Open Creator Search Insights and search for a topic you want to create about. Then look through the search results and check for a Content Gap.
A content gap can point you toward topics with search interest where there may be an opportunity for more relevant content.
Pick one specific angle, turn it into a clear video idea, and create around the search instead of guessing.
