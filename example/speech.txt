We wanted to test something different.

So we asked GPT and Luna to build a browser game from scratch.

Not a mockup. A real interactive game running directly in the browser.

The concept is Neon Escape: move through a procedural environment, dodge obstacles, collect energy and keep the score alive.

GPT built the initial game architecture: the loop, input state and browser rendering.

Luna then iterated on the mechanics, procedural spawning and the visual feedback.

The important part is that the game state is actually running in JavaScript.

Objects are generated during the loop, their positions change over time, and the player state drives the score and energy.

Then we added collision detection, so the gameplay is not just an animation pretending to be interactive.

Finally, we ran the result in Chromium and verified the complete loop.

So this isn't AI generating a picture of a game.

It's generating the software that runs the game.

GPT plus Luna, from prompt to playable browser prototype.
