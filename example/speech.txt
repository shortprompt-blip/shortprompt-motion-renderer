Instagram Edits is adding an AI assistant that can help explain what is working on your Reels. Instead of giving generic advice, it can use signals from your account, including views, likes, shares, follows and audience retention, alongside comments and Instagram trends. Open Edits and tap the assistant chat icon, if it is available on your account. Try asking: “Why did my best Reel outperform the others, and what should I test next?” The assistant can identify patterns and suggest new concepts, hooks, captions or audio ideas. Treat these as hypotheses, not guarantees. Compare the suggestions with your own analytics, test one change at a time, and measure the result. Availability may vary by region and account.
