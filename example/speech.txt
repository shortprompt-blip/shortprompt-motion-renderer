What if your PlayStation 5 could run an AI language model locally?

A new project called PS5LM Plus brings llama dot cpp to a jailbroken PS5 and uses the console's GPU.

The developer reports around twenty tokens per second with a quantized Qwen 3.8 model with twenty-seven billion parameters. Smaller models can be much faster.

But there's a catch. This is not an official PlayStation feature. You need a compatible console, a jailbreak, and homebrew software. It does not work on a normal, fully updated PS5.

The console can act as a local AI server, so other devices on your home network can chat with the model.

It's a fascinating experiment — not a reason to modify your main console.
