Want more chances of going viral on Instagram? Start with the Reel, not the hashtags. First, hook viewers immediately. Skip the introduction and give them a reason to keep watching. Second, focus on one idea per Reel. Third, use pattern interrupts: change the visual, add text, zoom, or movement before the video feels static. Fourth, deliver a clear payoff before the end. And finally, try creating a natural loop, so the ending leads smoothly back into the beginning. There’s no guaranteed viral formula, but these five changes can make your Reels much easier to watch.
