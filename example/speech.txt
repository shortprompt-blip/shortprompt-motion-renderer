Your OBS stream sounds doubled, echoey, or out of sync? You usually don't need new software. You need to find where the audio is being routed.

First, wear headphones while checking OBS. If you monitor the microphone through speakers, the mic can pick that sound up again and create echo.

Open Edit, then Advanced Audio Properties. Find the microphone or capture source. Set Audio Monitoring to Monitor Off if you don't need to hear it through OBS. If you do need monitoring, choose the correct headphones as your monitoring device, and avoid routing the same source back twice.

Now check duplicate sources. If your microphone is enabled both as a global audio device and as a separate source, you may be capturing it twice. Keep one intended input active, then make a short test recording.

For lip-sync delay, open Advanced Audio Properties and adjust Sync Offset in milliseconds on the source that is actually early or late. Don't guess: record a clap, review it, and change the offset in small steps.

The OneOdio Pro-10 is an optional wired headphone for monitoring your sound while you test. It won't remove echo or fix sync automatically; it helps you hear the problem.
