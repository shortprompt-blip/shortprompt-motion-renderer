Your microphone sounds distant, noisy, or uneven? Before buying anything, fix these five OBS settings.
First, select the correct microphone in the Audio Mixer and speak at your normal volume. Keep the mic close and at a consistent distance.
Open Filters on your microphone source. Add Noise Suppression and start with RNNoise for steady background sounds like PC fan noise. Too much suppression can damage your voice.
Next, use a Noise Gate only if noise is noticeable between sentences. Set it carefully so quiet words are not cut off.
Add a Compressor to control sudden loud peaks. Then place a Limiter last to help prevent clipping.
Record a short test with both quiet and loud speech, then listen back on headphones.
