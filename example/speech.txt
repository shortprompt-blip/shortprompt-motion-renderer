Your phone can shoot sharp video and still make you sound far away. That is because the built-in microphone is several feet from your voice, picking up the room along with you.

Here is a simple mobile audio setup using the RØDE Wireless Micro USB-C.

First, plug the USB-C receiver directly into your phone. Remove a thick case if it blocks the port. Take the transmitters out of the charging case and let them connect.

Clip one transmitter near your upper chest, with the microphone unobstructed. Keep fabric from rubbing against it. Outdoors, fit the supplied furry windshield to reduce wind hitting the microphone.

Before recording, make a short test. Speak at your normal volume, listen back with headphones, and check that your voice is clear without clipping. The on-screen waveform here is an illustrative animation, not a measured product test.

The kit has two transmitters, so you can mic two people. RØDE specifies 24-bit, 48-kilohertz audio, up to seven hours from a transmitter, and up to twenty-one hours with the charging case.

The USB-C version supports Android 11 and later, and iOS 14 and later. Check your phone and camera app before buying.
