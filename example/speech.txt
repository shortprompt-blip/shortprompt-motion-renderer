What if AI could predict what happens next in the physical world? NVIDIA Cosmos 3 is an open foundation model for physical AI. It brings together visual reasoning, world generation, and action prediction across inputs like text, images, and video. That matters for training robots, testing autonomous systems, and generating synthetic scenarios that would be difficult or expensive to capture in real life. It is not a robot by itself: it is a model developers can build into larger systems. The big shift is from describing a scene to helping systems reason about possible actions.
