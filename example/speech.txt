Europe has a new AI contender: Mistral Large 4. Mistral says it has around one trillion parameters, with 49 billion active at a time, and was trained on European infrastructure. The model targets coding, agent workflows, multimodal tasks, and areas like cybersecurity and finance. The key difference is open weights: organisations can gain more control over deployment. But size and launch claims do not prove it beats the best closed models. The real test is independent performance, total cost, and how reliably it handles real work. Can Europe compete? The benchmarks and real-world deployments will tell us.
