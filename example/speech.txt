Need a USB microphone for streaming or gaming? The FIFINE AM8 combines a dynamic capsule with USB and XLR connections. It uses a cardioid pickup pattern and has a specified frequency range of 50 hertz to 16 kilohertz. USB is the simple route: connect it to your computer, select fifine Microphone, then use the onboard gain and mute controls. You also get a headphone output for direct monitoring and RGB lighting. These onboard functions work in USB mode. XLR gives you an upgrade path to an audio interface or mixer. But the XLR cable is not included, and the mic's onboard controls and headphone monitoring are disabled. FIFINE recommends speaking into the top from about two to six inches away. Placement still matters; this is not automatic noise cancellation. The AM8 suits creators who want USB now and XLR later. Check the exact bundle and current offer in the description.
