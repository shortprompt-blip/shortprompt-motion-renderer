Want to edit Shorts faster in Final Cut Pro? Try these three practical habits. First, build your rough cut with the Magnetic Timeline. Remove pauses and weak takes before adding effects, so the story stays tight. Second, use captions to make spoken tips easier to follow with the sound off. Review the wording and timing before export. Third, create a vertical project and check the framing of every clip. Keep the subject and key text away from the edges where app controls may cover them.
