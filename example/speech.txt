A self-driving car must do more than spot objects. It needs to reason about unusual situations and choose what to do next. NVIDIA Alpamayo 2 Super is an open, thirty-four-billion-parameter reasoning vision-language-action model aimed at developing Level Four autonomous driving systems. It is designed to connect what the vehicle sees with reasoning, planning, and driving actions, while helping developers study difficult edge cases in simulation. This does not mean robotaxis are automatically safe or ready everywhere. It is a development model in a much larger safety and validation stack. Follow for more AI tools and creator tech.
