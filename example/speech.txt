Want to know where your YouTube views actually come from? Open YouTube Studio, select Analytics, then open the Content or Reach report for the video you want to understand. Find the traffic sources report. YouTube Search means people found you through search results. Browse Features includes places like Home and subscriptions. Suggested Videos shows traffic from recommendations beside or after other videos. External means a website or app linked to your video. Click a source to inspect its details, such as search terms or referring sites when available. These categories explain discovery, not whether a video is good by themselves.
