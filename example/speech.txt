YouTube can show you where content is missing.

Open YouTube Studio, go to Analytics, and select Trends.

Search for a topic your audience cares about.

Then look for a Content Gap.

A content gap means viewers may not be finding enough quality results for a specific search.

You might see a gap because there are no results, no exact matches, or the available videos are outdated, irrelevant, or low quality.

But don't treat a content gap as a guarantee of views.

Use it as an opportunity signal.

Find the search, understand what viewers need, then create a specific video that answers that need better.
