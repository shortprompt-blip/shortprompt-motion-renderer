If you’re a creator, don’t optimize only your content. Optimize your profile too. First, make your name searchable. Use your name plus what you actually create, so people can understand your niche. Second, make your bio promise specific. Tell visitors who you help and what they can expect from you. Third, pin your three best posts: an introduction, your strongest piece of content, and something that proves your expertise. Fourth, keep your profile picture simple and recognizable, especially on mobile. And finally, make your link support your main goal. Don’t send every visitor to five different places. Give them one clear next step. Your profile has one job: turn a profile visit into a reason to follow.
