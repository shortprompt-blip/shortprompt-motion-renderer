Claude Haiku 5.5 is Anthropic’s new small model, built for speed and lower-cost AI work. Anthropic says it costs around seventy-five percent less to run than Haiku 4.5. But what about coding? Its own benchmark table reports thirty-nine point two percent on Terminal-Bench 4.0, compared with zero percent for Haiku 4.5. That is a major jump on this test, not proof it wins every coding task. The real question is whether it solves your task reliably, fast, and cheaply. Test it on your own code before choosing.
