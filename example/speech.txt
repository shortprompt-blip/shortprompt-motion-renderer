You're probably using Microsoft Copilot the basic way. Try these seven practical hacks.

First, build better prompts with four parts: goal, context, source, and expected result.

Second, upload a PDF, spreadsheet, presentation, or image. Ask Copilot to extract key findings. Microsoft currently allows up to twenty files per conversation, with a fifty megabyte limit per file.

Third, don't start over. Refine the answer with follow-ups: make it shorter, turn it into a checklist, or explain what is missing.

Fourth, use Search mode when you need current web information, and open the citations to verify the claims.

Fifth, choose Think Deeper for complex questions when the option is available on your account.

Sixth, specify the audience, tone, format, and length you want.

And seventh, ground the answer in a specific source. Say: use only the attached document, and tell me if the answer isn't there.

Copilot works best when your instructions are clear and you verify important facts. Follow ShortPrompt for more AI tricks.
