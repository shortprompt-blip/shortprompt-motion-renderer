Want to create an Instagram carousel that mixes photos and videos? You can now build one in Edits. Open Projects, tap the plus button, and choose Carousel. Select the photos and videos you want to include, then arrange them on the canvas. Need another slide? Tap plus to add one, or use the shortcut on the final slide. To change the order, open a slide's three-dot menu and move it where you want. Use layouts to keep spacing consistent, then add text, audio, filters, or crops. When you're done, share the carousel directly to Instagram from Edits. One important note: Meta announced the feature for iOS first, with Android availability to follow. Check your version of the app before trying it.
