Want to edit your microphone and desktop audio independently? Record them on separate tracks in OBS Studio, while keeping one complete mix for easy playback.

Open Settings, then Output. Change Output Mode to Advanced and select the Recording tab. Enable Tracks 1, 2, and 3. Now open Edit, Advanced Audio Properties, or use the audio mixer menu.

Assign your microphone to Track 1 and Track 2. Assign Desktop Audio to Track 1 and Track 3. If your game is a separate source, enable Track 4 in Recording and assign the game to Tracks 1 and 4.

Here’s why this routing works. Track 1 contains the full mix, so it plays normally in most video players. Track 2 contains only the microphone, and Track 3 contains only desktop audio. You can lower, mute, or clean up those sources independently in your editor.

For recording, MKV is a sensible format because an unexpected shutdown is less likely to ruin the entire file. If your editor needs MP4, use OBS’s remux recordings feature after recording.

Now make a short test. Open the file in your editing software and check each track: the master mix, the mic alone, and desktop audio alone. Some ordinary players only play one track, so verify the tracks in an editor before changing your settings.
