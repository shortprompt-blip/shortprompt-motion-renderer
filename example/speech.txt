What if one AI prompt could turn a complex idea into a working 3D experience? Meet Gemini 4 Argon. Google says Argon is built for long, multi-step work, especially coding, research, and complex professional tasks. In one Google demonstration, Argon was given a short prompt and no assets or libraries. It wrote around five thousand lines of mathematical code to render a sunlit 3D library. Another example used Antigravity to build an interactive periodic table with animated atoms. The important detail: these are Google demonstrations combining models and tools, not proof that Argon alone creates every visual.
