STEP 03 / READ THE SOURCES

Each source answers a different question.

YouTube Search

Viewers found the video through YouTube search results. Inspect search terms when available.

Browse Features

Includes Home, subscriptions, Watch Later, Explore and other browsing surfaces.

Search suggests what people looked for. Browse shows where they encountered your content.
STEP 04 / EXPAND THE REPORT

Check recommendations and external links.

Suggested Videos

Traffic from recommendations beside or after other videos, plus qualifying video-description links.

External

Websites and apps that linked to or embedded the video.

Which video?Inspect the suggested-video details when the report provides them.
Which site?Inspect external sites and apps when those details are available.
STEP 05 / TURN DATA INTO A DECISION

Don't judge a source by views alone.

Compare discovery with watch time and retention to understand what happens after the click.
