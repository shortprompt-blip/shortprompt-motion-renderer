STEP 03 / READ THE SOURCES

Each source answers a different question.

YouTube Search

Viewers found the video through YouTube search results. Inspect search terms when available.

Browse Features

Includes surfaces such as Home, subscriptions, Watch Later, and Explore.

Search shows discovery through search results. Browse shows discovery through browsing surfaces.

STEP 04 / EXPAND THE REPORT

Check recommendations and external links.

Suggested Videos

Traffic from recommendations beside or after other videos. Check the report for details when available.

External

Websites and apps that linked to or embedded the video.

Which video?Inspect suggested-video details when the report provides them.
Which site?Inspect external sites and apps when details are available.

STEP 05 / TURN DATA INTO A DECISION

Don't judge a source by views alone.

Compare discovery with watch time and retention to understand what happens after the click.
